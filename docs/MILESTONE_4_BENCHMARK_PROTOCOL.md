# Milestone 4 frozen evaluation protocol

The machine-readable declaration is [milestone4_protocol_v2.json](../config/milestone4_protocol_v2.json). This is an experiment with **real Chennai road geography and simulated freight operations/outcomes**. It does not estimate field logistics effectiveness. The existing historical research cohorts, Milestone 3 results and B0–B4 source remain preserved. No observations from Brazil, Great Britain or another period become Chennai operational labels.

## Input declaration and independent assessment

The initial unused declaration exposed cross-split near-duplicate operation templates during an input-only audit, before fitting or final outcome generation. Its declaration and manifest are retained. Revision 2 uses actual, split-specific fleet sizes: training has 4/8 trucks, validation 5/9, calibration 6/10, and final assessment 7/11/12. These are constructed truck, driver and delivery resources, including the no-incident control, rather than unused metadata changes. Final fleet size 7 interpolates the training range; 11/12 extrapolate beyond it. This is **held-out fleet-size transfer**, not an IID random-snapshot assessment. The same bounded Chennai network, three prepared origin/destination pairs and scenario-family library remain shared.

The 64 training cases use seeds 1001–1008 across eight families. Validation and calibration each contain 24 cases, from seeds 2001–2003 and 3001–3003 respectively. Final inputs contain 40 cases, from seeds 4001–4004 across ten families, including two final-only larger resource combinations. Exposed Milestone 3 seeds 11/22/33/44/55 are excluded. All families of a seed, their trucks, nodes, adjacent frames and labels stay in one split. A snapshot is not an independent experimental replicate.

The independent [audit script](../scripts/audit_milestone4_leakage.py) checks actual resource counts, exact seed/family membership, seed ownership, exact configuration and nonempty schedule duplication, the declared primary/abstract groups, an independently reconstructed conservative group, and continuous near-neighbor equivalence across quantization boundaries. Seed, load, speed, names and exact incident-edge jitter cannot prove split isolation. An incident or restoration parameter is ignored by this audit when no corresponding event/resource exists. Six counterexample unit tests exercise these failure modes. A passing input-only audit authorizes development export. Model fitting additionally requires the development target audit to pass. Both gates bind the actual protocol, generation manifest, graph, feature schema and reviewed feature/label-source SHA-256 values; fitting rejects changed declarations or changed development corpus bytes.

After development export, the audit separately reconstructs every available ETA, SLA and 300-second resource-event target from authoritative outcomes, verifies stored null/masking parity, and checks that the feature graph has no realized labels. It validates each exact inference projection against the recursive private-schedule/outcome rejection contract, and checks complete truck and resource target membership. Incomplete future windows remain null even when part of the window contains an observed event; an absorbing completed run can legitimately close the future window. Missing arrivals or deliveries do not become invented timestamps or negative labels.

No final outcomes are opened during input audit, training, normalization, hyperparameter/epoch selection or calibration. The model bundle freezes model IDs, feature/network hashes, source development corpus hashes and serialized artifact hashes before final generation. Java final generation and final policy execution require that frozen bundle and the exact development corpus. Assessment checks the exact frozen bundle and generation/protocol hashes again. A failed experiment or restricted model variant is reported rather than silently replaced. Once final outcomes have been inspected, a new training study requires a newly declared independent assessment.

## Prediction assessment

Evaluate ETA only before actual road arrival, and SLA only before completion of at least one assigned delivery. ETA is remaining road-arrival time; delivery SLA uses actual dock completion against its configured deadline. Future resource impact means a **new recorded adverse event onset** in the following 300 seconds, for the six declared runtime codes. It does not mean incident causation or every continuing queue tick.

Report ETA MAE/RMSE/bias; SLA and event precision/recall/F1, PR AUC, ROC AUC, Brier score, class counts and confusion matrices; probability calibration bins; ETA interval coverage; error by scenario family and current exposure severity; and actual CPU inference latency. Single-class AUC is undefined. Known-only deterministic ETA baselines retain their own explicit coverage denominator. Compare deterministic distance/speed, rule ETA, slack/rule dock forecasts, graph risk and training-only means/prevalences on shared example IDs where each baseline is defined.

Only training cases fit model weights and GNN normalization. Validation selects the predeclared XGBoost alternatives and GNN epoch. Calibration is separate and fits probability calibration and residual intervals. No final-based thresholds or feature changes are permitted. Residual intervals use correlated snapshots and are descriptive simulation uncertainty; their nominal level is not a guaranteed field coverage statement.

The no-graph XGBoost variant removes only the three explicit graph-summary fields; it retains deterministic rule ETA and resource context. The GNN consumes additional typed resource attributes, so XGBoost versus GNN is not a pure architecture-only comparison. Static versus temporal GNN is the narrower history ablation. Temporal development frames have irregular gaps whereas the policy samples actual past state at a 10-second cadence; encoded elapsed time does not eliminate this deployment shift.

## Paired downstream policy comparison

Every policy receives the identical initial configuration, private exogenous event schedule and simulator physics for a final case. Original B0–B4 use their existing code. B0 deliberately takes no action. The common available action types are NONE, CONTINUE, WAIT, LOCAL_REROUTE and GLOBAL_REROUTE; dock reservation adjustment is excluded for this controlled comparison. Candidate feasibility, incoming-turn restrictions, occupied-edge safety and current-knowledge rules remain authoritative. Private future incidents and restoration events are injected by the experiment process and never supplied to a predictor or planning copy.

Use at most six decision slots per run, 30-second periodic checks, 10-second observation cadence and at most 1800 simulated seconds per candidate rollout. Announced incidents and newly observed resource risk may trigger checks between periodic checks. The existing policies retain their distinct eligibility/selection rules, so an equal maximum budget does not imply equal realized decision counts. The declared B5 variants are B5_RULE_V1, B5_XGB_V1, B5_XGB_NO_GRAPH_V1, B5_GNN_STATIC_V1, B5_GNN_TEMPORAL_V1 and B5_XGB_CF_V1. Omit only untrained/unvalidated model IDs, record why, and retain all adverse results for eligible variants. With every declared model available this gives 11 policies × 40 cases = 440 policy runs.

Direct B5 scores use fixed weights: .60 mean SLA-failure probability, .25 clipped mean predicted lateness/1800, .10 mean new-impact probability and .05 positive extra-distance ratio. Candidate-specific min/max normalization is forbidden. B5_RULE applies that same algorithm with deterministic estimates, making it the control for changing the policy objective. B5_XGB_CF adds equally weighted direct-ML and executed-outcome scores plus the predeclared unfinished-delivery penalty. It executes the same feasible candidates and horizon as B4 without model-assisted pruning. This CF variant consumes more inference work than B4 and more simulation work than direct B5; report these actual costs instead of asserting equal wall-clock compute.

Development training trajectories have no policy interventions. Rerouted/waiting candidate states may therefore lie outside the training distribution. These predictors estimate associative simulated outcomes, not identified causal effects of actions. Actual final policy outcomes, constraint audits and fallback counts decide whether prediction changes improved decisions. Better supervised prediction alone cannot establish prevention effectiveness.

Retain per-case SLA violations/rate, unfinished deliveries, total actual delivery lateness, downstream affected resources, reroutes, travel/extra distance, modeled operational cost, recovery time with censoring, hard constraint violations and decision computation time. Also record inference requests/failures, deterministic fallback decisions, candidate/rollout counts, service time, execution wall time and memory measurement scope. A model outage falls back for the complete decision, and must appear in its run evidence. Java heap-pool peak sums exclude Python/GPU/native memory and are not simultaneous process RSS.

Primary descriptive contrasts are B5_XGB_V1−B3, B5_XGB_V1−B4, B5_XGB_V1−B5_RULE_V1 and B5_XGB_CF_V1−B5_XGB_V1. Graph/history ablations compare the predeclared paired variants. Calculate each case's paired difference first, then average its ten family differences within each final seed; bootstrap those four whole seed clusters 2000 times using bootstrap seed 4016 for 95% percentile intervals. Keep the same seed resample for all cases in a cluster. With only four seed clusters intervals have low resolution and cannot justify a broad significance claim. No multiplicity correction is declared; all intervals and comparisons are descriptive. Report per-family effects and absolute values alongside averages. Missing run pairs cannot enter a contrast unnoticed.

The reusable [policy summarizer](../scripts/summarize_milestone4_policies.py) reads only completed raw outcomes after final assessment. It fails if any declared scenario/policy pair is absent, duplicated, drawn from a different checksum-bound experiment, or exceeds the declared action/decision limits. It writes `reports/MILESTONE_4_POLICY_BENCHMARK.csv` with actual per-run metrics and `reports/MILESTONE_4_POLICY_BENCHMARK.json` with absolute values, family breakdowns, paired seed-cluster intervals, omissions, failure/fallback counts and raw evidence hashes. Two counterexample tests verify whole-seed resampling and rejection of missing pairs. The summarizer does not manufacture missing outcomes or fit models.

Before any final outcome generation, freeze the executable policy implementation as well as its declared formula: record source and compiled-class SHA-256 maps for `MlPolicyRunner`, `MlPolicyCli`, `MlInferenceClient`, the original historical runner and simulator action/physics dependencies in the experiment execution journal. The final policy runner must use the frozen source/class versions. The pre-assessment `MlPolicyRunner` correction makes non-counterfactual alternatives feasible only when their authoritative validator reports no hard violation; this version is the one to freeze. No outcome or selected-model score was used for that correction. The model bundle and policy execution journal identify distinct artifacts; a trained weight hash alone does not freeze Java policy behavior.

## Existing B4 diagnosis and stop boundary

The preserved [Milestone 3 diagnostic evidence](../reports/milestone4_b4_diagnostics.json) records decision-budget saturation and identical candidate outcome vectors. In both comparison modes, 21 decisions contained unfinished forecast alternatives at driver-availability clocks 0 and 10 seconds; a private future driver restoration was unavailable to the planner. These data do not demonstrate that a shorter rollout horizon caused the issue. No selected candidate had more forecast SLA failures than another feasible candidate, or greater forecast lateness among candidates tied at minimum SLA failures. The historical traces therefore do not establish a within-forecast SLA/lateness ranking inversion.

Four new development-only B4 variants keep distinct identifiers: six decisions/1800-second reference, 12 decisions/1800 seconds, six decisions/900 seconds and six decisions/3600 seconds. Run these on the two declared validation seeds and two declared families (16 outcomes). Preserve every result. Do not rewrite or silently retune historical B4, or reuse these development diagnostics as independent final evidence.

Milestone 4 ends after actual model fitting, frozen prediction assessment, paired policy assessment, safe inference integration and reproducible reporting. It introduces no RL, LLM-generated decisions, blockchain or unvalidated real-world effectiveness claim.
